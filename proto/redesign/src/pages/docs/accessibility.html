What every piece does

  • Works with the keyboard alone, with a focus ring that is never removed.
  • Reflows at 400% zoom, down to a width of 320 pixels.
  • Holds in forced colors and under reduced motion.
  • Never uses color as the only signal of a state.
  • Takes its name from the page: a label, visible text, then ARIA as a last resort.
