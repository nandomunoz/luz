Five homes. Nothing in common.

Five experiments for the home page of LUZ, each with its own idea, motion and scroll. Each one is a single folder, vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no library.

  1. 01PrismWebGL · 3DA glass prism in the dark splits a beam of light into the three packages as you scroll.
  2. 02CityCSS 3DAn isometric city of components, flown over by a camera on your scroll.
  3. 03DarkroomLight · maskYour pointer is a lamp in a dark room; scroll until dawn and the interface is real.
  4. 04SourceScroll · morphThe page starts as its own HTML and renders itself as you read.
  5. 05BroadsheetHorizontal · printA special edition newspaper, read sideways, with pages that turn.
The designed site is at proto/sitepro.
