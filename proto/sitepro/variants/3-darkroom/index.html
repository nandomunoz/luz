Blue hour — the switches

Three packages. Three switches.

They stack. Each one lights its own layer of the page.

  1. Colour floods in.

    Design tokens as CSS custom properties. Light, dark and high contrast. Ceiling: 5 KB.

  2. The bare HTML gets its styles.

    Elements styled with no class, layout objects, 29 components, optional JS. Ceiling: 11.5 KB.

  3. The patterns assemble.

    41 site-wide pieces and page patterns for landing, blog, app and docs sites. One stylesheet each, no bundle.
