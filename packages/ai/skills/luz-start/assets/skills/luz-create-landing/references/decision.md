# Deciding the type

Two types:

- **Standard landing.** Presents something and leads the visitor to a step that happens later or elsewhere: buy, book, request a quote, sign up.
- **Landing with an integrated service.** The product lives inside the page. The visitor uses it there, before signing up or paying.

## The deciding question

> Can the visitor get the real result of the product on this page, in under a minute, without signing up or paying?

Yes: integrated service. No: standard.

## Five checks

Answer yes or no. Four or five yeses: integrated service. Two or fewer: standard. Three: read the doubtful cases, and ask the user if it is still open.

| # | Check | Yes points to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is the offer digital and does it run in the browser (input, process, result)? | Integrated service |
| 2 | Does the first use take seconds, with no account, payment or setup? | Integrated service |
| 3 | Is the result delivered on the same page (download, copy, view)? | Integrated service |
| 4 | Do people search for it with an action verb ("convert PDF to Word", "calculate mortgage", "generate QR")? | Integrated service |
| 5 | Can the owner afford free trials (compute, limits, abuse)? | Integrated service |

## Signals

| Signal | Standard | Integrated service |
|---|---|---|
| What is offered | Physical product, catalogue, service done by people, software that needs an account or installation | Web tool, converter, calculator, generator, analyzer |
| Goal of the page | The visitor takes the next step (cart, form, booking) | The visitor uses the product now |
| Where the value happens | After the click, outside the landing | On the landing, on the first screen |
| Lead of the hero | Headline, benefit and button | The tool widget |
| CTA text | "Buy", "Add to cart", "Book", "Request a demo" | The verb of the tool: "Convert", "Generate", "Calculate" |
| Main brake | "Is it worth the price?" | "What happens to my data?" and "Where is the catch?" |
| Arguments | Emotional: desire, pain, transformation | Operational: speed, limits, formats, privacy |
| Social proof | Star reviews, customer photos, press | Usage counter, reviews about time saved, client logos |
| Monetization | Direct sale or lead capture | Freemium: free with limits, Pro plan, API |
| Search that brings visitors | Buy, compare, know the brand | Get a specific task done |

## Doubtful cases

- **SaaS that needs an account.** If a useful slice works without signing up (one file, one calculation), integrated service with visible limits. If not, standard with a "Try it free" CTA.
- **Interactive demo that is not the real product.** Standard. The demo is a block inside "How it works", not the hero.
- **Calculator or configurator as a hook to sell something else** (mortgage, insurance, quote). Integrated service at the top, and the close of the page leads to the commercial step.
- **Mock-up without a backend.** If the widget can be emulated in the browser with JavaScript, it counts as integrated service.
- **Physical product with a customizer** (engraving, colours, sizes). Standard. The customizer belongs to the product page and does not replace the hero.

## What changes, section by section

| # | Standard | Integrated service |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Header with navigation, search, account and cart | Minimal header: tools, pricing, API, sign in |
| 2 | Hero: social proof, benefit headline, bullets, CTA | Hero with the working widget |
| 3 | The transformation in 3 steps | Privacy, security and speed bar |
| 4 | Best sellers showcase | Operational advantages in a grid |
| 5 | Emotional reinforcement blocks | How it works in 3 steps |
| 6 | Social proof: reviews and photos | Directory of use cases and related tools |
| 7 | Product categories | Social proof and technical backing |
| 8 | Purchase FAQ (shipping, returns) | Pro plans and API |
| 9 | Conversion close | Technical FAQ (limits, privacy, formats) |
| 10 | Footer with newsletter, legal links and payment methods | CTA back to the widget and footer in columns |
