# Anatomy of a standard landing

Written for a shop or a brand with a catalogue. With a single product or a service, drop the showcase (4) and the categories (7) and keep the rest.

Every review, rating, logo and number below is real or is left out. See the principles in `SKILL.md`.

## 1. Header

- Logo, left or centred.
- Navigation menu with the main links.
- Search, reachable from anywhere.
- Account and cart.
- Optional: top bar with short advantages (free shipping, guarantee).

## 2. Hero

Communicates the value in seconds, without scrolling.

- Social proof above the headline: the average rating, if there is one.
- Headline with the value proposition, centred on the main benefit.
- Subheadline and three bullets with the key benefits.
- One CTA that stands out by contrast. Only one.
- Optional: authority bar below ("As recommended by", press logos).

## 3. The transformation

- Short text on what changes for the customer.
- Three steps with simple icons: purchase, delivery or use, result.

## 4. Product showcase

- "Best sellers": three or four products with image, price and rating.
- "Add to cart" on each one.

## 5. Emotional reinforcement blocks

Placed between sections to repeat the offer.

- Headline that speaks to a desire or a pain of the customer.
- Image of the product in use, one short testimonial and the CTA again.

## 6. Social proof

- Quotes from real reviews with their score.
- Three customer photos (round) or icons when there are no photos.
- CTA at the end of the section.

## 7. Product categories

For the visitor who does not know yet what to buy.

- Headline that introduces the range.
- Three categories with image, name and a link to their page.

## 8. FAQ

- Accordion with the purchase objections: shipping, returns, use, guarantee.
- Optional: gallery of customer content below, to show community.

## 9. Conversion close

Last chance before the footer: rating, emotive headline, main CTA and one micro-review.

## 10. Footer

- Newsletter signup: email field and a clear button.
- Columns of links: site map, legal policies, contact.
- Social profiles.
- Payment method logos and the site name.
