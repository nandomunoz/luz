The sidebar is the colour of the page in light, in high contrast and in the morning and sunset moments: `--luz-sidebar-bg` is the page's own colour there, its links are read on it, and the current link is a filled one. Dark keeps its darker panel. A theme of your own that set the sidebar tokens for a dark panel in light keeps working; `packages/core/src/themes/_template.css` shows the new defaults.
